Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Roome ("Roome", "we", "us") is a roommate and room-matching app for people in Australia. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

1. Information we collect

  • Account details: your mobile number and/or email address, used to sign you in.
  • Profile information you provide: name, date of birth, gender, the area you want to live in, budget, move-in timing, length of stay, lifestyle preferences, prompt answers and photos.
  • Room listings: if you list a room, its title, suburb, price, availability, description and photos.
  • Messages: the messages you send to other users through the app.
  • Location: the location you choose by tapping the map when setting your preferred area. We do not track your device location in the background.
  • Optional verification and connections: if you choose to verify, we receive the result of that check (for example, that your email is a university address, that a LinkedIn account was connected, or that an identity check passed). We do not receive or store the identity document images you submit to our identity-check provider.
  • Technical information: basic device and usage data needed to run and secure the app, such as error reports.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account and send sign-in codes.
  • To show your profile and listings to other users and suggest compatible matches.
  • To deliver messages between users who have matched.
  • To display verification badges you have chosen to earn.
  • To keep the community safe, handle reports and enforce our Terms of Use.
  • To maintain and improve the app.

3. Who we share it with

Other users can see the profile information and photos you choose to show. We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with service providers that help us run Roome, who may only use it for that purpose:

  • Supabase — database, authentication and file storage.
  • Twilio (or a similar SMS provider) — delivering sign-in codes by text message.
  • Stripe — optional identity verification, if you choose to use it.
  • LinkedIn — only if you choose to connect your LinkedIn account.
  • Google Maps — displaying maps and looking up the name of a location you select.

We may also disclose information where required by law or to protect the safety of our users.

4. Overseas storage

Some of our service providers store or process information outside Australia, including in the United States. By using Roome you understand your information may be handled overseas by these providers under their own privacy and security commitments.

5. Keeping your information

We keep your information while your account is active. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete or de-identify your personal information within a reasonable time, except where we must keep it for legal, security or fraud-prevention reasons.

6. Your rights and choices

  • You can view and edit most of your profile information in the app.
  • You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, or to delete your account, by emailing us at the address below. We will respond within a reasonable time.
  • Verification and account connections are optional, and you can ask us to remove them.

7. Age requirement

Roome is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has created an account, please contact us and we will remove it.

8. Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections and access controls. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Complaints

If you have a privacy concern, contact us first and we will try to resolve it. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new version here and update the date above. Significant changes will be notified in the app.
